## Failure and minimal fix

Original ask-riot said to name the saved next step and stance said an active checkpoint resumes that step. A choose checkpoint with seven objections therefore produces a premature choice question if read literally. General instructions to request missing outputs suggested a safer interpretation but did not explicitly override the cursor. Minimal fix: treat cursor as a hint, validate the unique author/rival pairs and Cop verdict, rewind to the earliest missing prerequisite, and preserve all valid outputs. Parent implemented that rule; the same preserved input then produced debate-first behavior and nine unique pairs.

## Boundaries and remaining ambiguities

- Revision research was explicitly seeded synthetic test material. This run checks changed-product propagation, preservation, choices and persistence, not live competitor research quality or venue truth. Candidate statements of comparative exclusivity were deliberately marked unverified. The resulting test manifesto is not a publishable marketing certification.
- The original VibeCheck fixture has broad unsupported competitive/platform assertions, and its safe baseline includes category-banned wording. Reusing it tests the safe-baseline choice branch but does not validate those claims. A practical-line factual review before public writing remains necessary.
- The revision fixture is legacy: its old success signal/review point is absent and its old debate is prose, with incomplete candidate provenance. Those historical limitations were preserved, not reconstructed. Fresh v2 received all nine keyed objections and an explicit synthetic outcome/review point.
- New taste questions are stored as separate message entries in one transcript artifact; they were not sent to the real user. The synthetic answers are test inputs, not approvals from the human using Codex.
- No commit-recovery defect was observed. The crash was an intentional stop between two real file executions, not a killed process. No concurrent writer, changed-base conflict, or hardware durability guarantee was tested.
