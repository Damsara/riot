## Evidence

For every factual product, competitor, metric, quotation, or first-person experience claim, record the supporting repository path, observed result, builder statement, or source URL and what it establishes. Remove unsupported factual claims, narrow them to supported scope, or ask for the missing evidence. An `unverified` marker is a research gap, not permission to put a disputed fact into ready copy. Preserve exact quotations and attribution. Apply the canon's practical line to the final text.
