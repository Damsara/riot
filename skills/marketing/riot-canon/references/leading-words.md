> The twelve words riot thinks with. Each is a concept the model already knows, or one riot coins for a shape it needs; the definition here fixes which sense riot means.

# Leading words

## manifesto
The direction file, `docs/riot/manifesto.md`. The contract every later piece is written from: nothing ships that cannot be traced back to it.

## category default
What every competitor in the product's category says, how they say it, and the formats they use, written as a banned list. Not a description of the market: a list of sentences no piece may write unchanged.

## taste
The builder's own reactions to real examples (love, hate, nothing), plus the builder's own phrases (the bar description, the origin story, what annoys them about the category), recorded verbatim. Taste is why two products with the same manifesto shape produce different pieces.

## candidate and decoy
A candidate is one proposed direction in the manifesto's Direction shape; four are generated per stance run. The decoy is the one candidate written to be the safest, most competent direction a good agency would sell, included so the debate has a safe baseline to lose against.

## provocation
The one-paragraph brief an unsafe candidate's author starts from, built from a specific research finding: a named complaint, a gap between the category's promise and its reviews, a venue in-joke. A brief with a source, not a slogan.

## recurring move
The one thing every piece for a product does: a running joke, a format, an enemy it keeps naming. It is what makes a builder's fifth piece recognizably the same product as their first.

## the Cop
The adversary. Reads as a bored member of the target audience: says where it scrolled, where it stopped, cannot be argued with. Judges both the four candidates and every piece; going last and being unappealable is the point.

## piece
One unit of content, written by a format skill to `docs/riot/pieces/`. Always traced back to the manifesto's Direction and logged in its Log section.

## occasion
Why a piece is being written now: launch, feature, rant, reply to something. Every piece names one; it is what makes a piece specific instead of generic.

## the practical line
No false claims about the product, no fabricated reviews or testimonials or press quotes, no attacks on private individuals. Not a taste judgement: a rule that gets a builder banned from the venues the research found.

## kind of unsafe
The specific way a chosen direction takes a side, derived from this product and this builder's taste, never picked from a menu. Two builders with the same product get two different answers.

## what it is willing to lose
The readers a direction accepts losing, and the kind of comment it accepts drawing, named up front in section 5. A direction that cannot name what it loses has not actually chosen a side.
