# Venues

A launch that breaks a venue's rule gets removed, shadowed, or banned. Check a launch piece row by row against its venue before it ships, and rewrite whatever breaks a rule.

| Venue | Rule that gets launches removed | Current state (2026-09) | What works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Show HN | Title must begin with "Show HN"; must be something people can run or try; no landing pages, sign-up walls, waitlists, newsletters, fundraisers, or minor version bumps unless a major overhaul (https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html); never solicit upvotes, comments, or friends to promote it (https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html). | Volume tripled in 2025 to about 200 Show HNs a day; median score 2 points, 50+ points is the top 6% (https://danfking.github.io/blog/2026/04/23/show-hn-by-the-numbers/). A voting-ring detector suppresses coordinated voting patterns before the front page (https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22761897). Monday 00:00 UTC has the best odds, `unverified` (https://trilogydata.dev/blog/bad_at_hackernews.html). | Link the repo, not a landing page. Explain how and why it was built in the first comment. Substance over polish; be around to discuss it (https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html). |
| Product Hunt | Featuring is curated: useful, novel, high craft; waitlists, directories, templates, and monetisation-first minimal products are excluded (https://www.producthunt.com/launch/how-product-hunt-works). Personal accounts only, no paid hunters or traffic, no self-promotion in comments (https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/3615694-community-guidelines); vote rings and bought votes are named violations (https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/11869098-how-does-product-hunt-ensure-fair-voting-and-prevent-spam-or-vote-manipulation). | About 10% of launches are featured; unfeatured launches get a fraction of the traffic. New day resets 12:01 AM PT (https://www.producthunt.com/launch/how-product-hunt-works). Tue-Thu has the highest traffic and competition; Fri-Sun reportedly better odds for the #1-of-day badge, `unverified` (https://www.producthunt.com/p/general/what-s-the-best-day-to-launch-on-product-hunt-2). | Credibility and feedback more than customers. Needs an audience brought along; a landing page belongs here, not on HN. |
| Reddit (general) | Be a redditor with a product, not a product with a Reddit account. Participate for weeks before promoting; the informal norm is no more than 1 in 10 posts self-promotional (https://redship.io/blog/reddit-self-promotion-rules). Many subreddits restrict self-promotion to designated threads and remove bare link drops. | Sitewide spam policy targets content whose primary purpose is promotion rather than participation; individual subreddits enforce their own, often stricter, rules (https://redship.io/blog/reddit-self-promotion-rules). Second-hand, verify against the subreddit's rules page before use: direct fetches of reddit.com were blocked during research. | A story post without a link, answering questions in the comments. |
| r/SideProject | No published formal rules page found; runs on cultural norms. Bare links with no explanation get ignored, downvoted, or removed (https://rankhog.com/subreddits/sideproject). | Norm is to share what you built, explain it, and ask for specific feedback. Second-hand, verify against the subreddit's rules page before use. | "I built X, here's what I learned," answered in the comments. |
| r/webdev | Commercial promotion and solicitation are prohibited; noncommercial project showoffs are limited to Showoff Saturday (https://www.redditgrowthdb.com/database/subreddits/webdev). Standalone self-promo posts outside it are removed regardless of framing. | Enforced by subreddit moderators, not sitewide. Second-hand, verify against the subreddit's rules page before use. | A Showoff Saturday post that leads with the technical build, not the pitch. |
| r/startups | Self-promotion is restricted to a stickied "Share Your Startup" thread; standalone promotional posts are removed on sight. Every post requires correct flair or it is auto-removed; blog links need prior mod approval (https://www.redditmaster.com/subreddit-rules/startups). | Enforced by subreddit moderators, not sitewide. Second-hand, verify against the subreddit's rules page before use. | A comment inside the designated thread, full post body included, not just a link. |
| Indie Hackers | Posts should help others learn, talk shop, or discover things, not promote; reframe a self-focused ask into a generally useful question (https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tips-for-making-successful-posts-on-indie-hackers-b04454a57a). Individual groups can set their own posting guidelines, shown before submission (https://www.indiehackers.com/product/indie-hackers/added-support-for-group-posting-guidelines--MHDC04726tqs6fDr-W5). | The r/indiehackers Reddit offshoot (a separate community) allows self-promotion once per product with "SHOW IH" flair and requires proof for MRR claims, `unverified` secondary source (https://gofindevo.com/subreddits/indiehackers). | "I built X, here's what I learned" outperforms "check out X" (https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tips-for-making-successful-posts-on-indie-hackers-b04454a57a). Skip the company name in the title unless it's already well known. |
| X, build in public | No platform rule; reach is the constraint. Links in the post body cut reach; third-party estimates of the cut conflict, from roughly 30% to 90%, `unverified` (https://www.niemanlab.org/2026/04/do-links-hurt-news-publishers-on-twitter-our-analysis-suggests-yes/). Engagement-bait solicitation ("reply and I'll follow you back") repeated three or more times pulls accounts from the creator revenue-share program (https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies). | The most-used channel among bootstrapped apps; the audience is mostly other makers, not customers. Failure posts outperform win posts. | A warm audience for the other launches. Never the only channel. |
| Newsletters (sponsored) | Paid placement; price scales with list size (https://github.com/jackbridger/developer-newsletters). | Niche developer lists from a few hundred dollars per issue; large ones in the thousands. | Only after the evidence gate, and only when lifetime value supports it. |
| Registries and directories (npm, skills.sh, marketplaces, plugin stores) | Listing rules vary; the artifact is the README and the install count (https://vercel.com/changelog/introducing-skills-the-open-agent-skills-ecosystem). | skills.sh discovers by search and install count; Claude Code's official marketplace accepts plugins by review. | A product inside an ecosystem gets customers from the ecosystem; the README is the landing page. |
| GitHub Trending | Undocumented; relative star velocity within a language and window, `unverified`. | A front-page HN post is the usual driver; the effect decays within a week, `unverified`. | A consequence of another launch, not a launch. |
| Personal network | None. | The most common source of first customers in every dataset consulted, `unverified` aggregate claim. | Named people who match the job story, asked one at a time. |

Three rules repeat across every venue above regardless of channel: no engagement bait (soliciting votes, likes, comments, or replies as a substitute for content), no fabricated social proof (bought votes, follower farms, sock puppets, unproven revenue claims), and disclose the piece's origin where the venue asks (https://dev.to/code-of-conduct). Treat every `unverified` mark above as directional, not load-bearing, when checking a piece against its row.
