# launch

## Format question

Ask which venue: Product Hunt, Show HN, a subreddit, or Indie Hackers.

## The venue check

riot-canon's venues.md is the rule table; this file does not duplicate it. Check the piece row by row against riot-canon's venues.md and rewrite any line that breaks a rule. A Reddit row marked second-hand is verified against the subreddit's own rules page before the piece ships.

## Draft rules

1. Show HN: link the repo, not a landing page; the first comment explains how and why it was built.
2. Product Hunt: the tagline is under sixty characters; the first comment is the founder story, not a feature list.
3. Reddit: the post is a story without a link; a link goes in the comments only if the subreddit allows it.
4. Indie Hackers: the piece carries real numbers and what went wrong, not just what worked.

## The venue-check record

The piece's transcript carries one of these per rule checked:

```
Venue: <name>
Rule: <the rule from venues.md>
Line checked: <the piece's line>
Result: passes | rewritten to "<the new line>"
```

## What gets you punished

- Show HN: soliciting upvotes, comments, or friends to promote it; a landing page, sign-up wall, waitlist, newsletter, fundraiser, or minor version bump gets recategorized or killed as off-topic; coordinated voting patterns are suppressed by the ring detector before the front page.
- Product Hunt: paid hunters or traffic, self-promotion in comments, and vote rings or bought votes get the launch delisted or unfeatured.
- Reddit: exceeding the informal one-in-ten self-promotion ratio, a standalone promotional post outside a subreddit's designated thread, and a bare link with no explanation all get removed or downvoted.
- Indie Hackers: a post that exists only to promote, with no learning or discussion angle, gets removed or buried; on the r/indiehackers offshoot, an MRR or revenue claim with no proof gets removed.
