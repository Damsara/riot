> Rules for a video script on YouTube long-form, Shorts, TikTok, or Reels: duration caps, monetisation thresholds, and what each platform punishes.

# video

## Format question

Ask one thing: talking-head or screen recording. The platform (YouTube long-form, Shorts, TikTok, or Reels) is the one the manifesto's section 2 venues name; where section 2 names none, take short vertical (Shorts, TikTok, Reels share one script) and say so.

## YouTube long-form

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Duration cap | No hard maximum in principle; up to 12 hours or 256 GB, whichever comes first, for verified accounts. Unverified accounts are capped at 15 minutes per upload until phone-number verification | https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/71673?hl=en |
| Monetisation threshold | 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 public watch hours in the trailing 12 months, no active strikes, 2-Step Verification, AdSense linked | https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en |
| Monetisation threshold, incoming | From Feb 1, 2027, new applicants need 8,000 watch hours (long-form path) or 20 million qualified Shorts views (90 day path) | https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en |
| Hook window | The official "Intro" retention metric measures percent of audience still watching after the first 30 seconds; a literal "first 15 seconds" cutoff is widely repeated but no YouTube source states it, unverified | https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en |

## What gets you punished

- YouTube: malicious clickbait, a title or thumbnail promising something the video doesn't deliver, draws escalating enforcement; three thumbnail-policy strikes in 90 days terminates the channel.
- YouTube Shorts: reused or non-original clips (unedited reposts, compilations with no added narrative) are named as excluded from Shorts monetisation entirely.
- TikTok: a repost that still shows another platform's watermark stays up but is ineligible for the For You feed.
- Instagram Reels: a third-party watermark, or an account that crosses roughly ten reposts of unoriginal content in a 30 day window, is cut from recommendation to non-followers.
- Every platform above: fake or bot-driven views or engagement toward a monetisation threshold fail eligibility review when the account is checked.

## Script shape

```
| On screen | Said aloud | Seconds |
|---|---|---|
| <the hook, on screen> | <the hook, said aloud> | <n> |
| <shot 2, on screen> | <shot 2, said aloud> | <n> |
```

The first row is the hook.

## Draft rules

1. The first row carries the opinion; a script that opens on a logo, a title card, or a scene-setting line before the opinion has buried its own hook.
2. A talking-head script keeps the builder's phrasing verbatim in the "said aloud" column; paraphrasing the builder's own words for polish is a rewrite, not a script.
3. A screen recording shows the product doing the thing the recurring move names, not a narrator describing it; the "on screen" column names the exact screen, click, or state, not "the app."
4. Every claim in the table carries a source URL or an `unverified` mark; carry the mark forward into the piece the way the tables above do.
